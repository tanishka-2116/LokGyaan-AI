# Project Requirements

## Project Title
LokGaayan AI – Preserving India’s Folk Voice through Artificial Intelligence

## Problem Statement
India has a rich heritage of folk music, oral traditions, regional storytelling, and local cultural expressions. However, much of this cultural knowledge is undocumented, scattered, or at risk of being lost due to modernization, language barriers, and lack of digital preservation. There is no unified, intelligent platform to document, understand, and promote India’s diverse folk traditions in an accessible way.

## Proposed Solution
LokGaayan AI is an AI-powered cultural preservation platform that uses Generative AI to document, analyze, and present India’s folk music and oral traditions. The system enables users to explore regional folk content, understand cultural context, and preserve traditional knowledge digitally for future generations.

## Target Users
- Folk artists and cultural practitioners  
- Students and researchers  
- Cultural organizations and NGOs  
- Educators and institutions  
- General public interested in Indian heritage  

## Functional Requirements
- AI-based generation and explanation of folk songs and stories  
- Region-wise and language-wise cultural categorization  
- Contextual interpretation of folk lyrics and traditions  
- Educational summaries for learners and researchers  
- Simple and accessible user interaction  

## Non-Functional Requirements
- High availability and reliability  
- Ethical and respectful handling of cultural content  
- Scalability for future data expansion  
- Secure handling of user inputs  

## Tools & Technologies
- Generative AI (Kiro)  
- Cloud Platform (AWS – conceptual)  
- GitHub for version control  
- Presentation (PPT/PDF)  

## Expected Impact
- Preservation of endangered folk traditions  
- Increased awareness of India’s cultural diversity  
- Digital documentation of oral heritage  
- Support for cultural education and research  

## Future Scope
- Multilingual and regional language support  
- Audio-based folk song analysis  
- Collaboration with cultural institutions  
- Mobile application for wider reach  
- Integration with national cultural archives
