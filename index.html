Makar Sankranti

Region: Maharashtra, Karnataka, UP, Bihar

Makar Sankranti marks the Sun’s northward movement and the end of winter. In Maharashtra, people exchange Tilgul and wish sweetness in relationships.

Food: Tilgul, puran poli

Clothing: Black sarees with colorful borders

Pongal

Region: Tamil Nadu

Pongal is a four-day harvest festival thanking the Sun God, farmers, cattle, and nature. Rice overflowing in pots symbolizes abundance.

Food: Sweet Pongal, Ven Pongal

Clothing: Veshti, silk sarees

Lohri

Region: Punjab, Haryana

Lohri is celebrated around bonfires to mark the winter harvest. Folk songs, Bhangra, and community bonding define the festival.

Food: Revri, peanuts, popcorn

Magh Bihu (Bhogali Bihu)

Region: Assam

Magh Bihu marks the end of the harvest season with community feasts and traditional rice-based dishes.

Food: Pitha, laru

Uttarayan

Region: Gujarat

Uttarayan celebrates the Sun’s northward journey and is famous for kite flying and rooftop gatherings.

Food: Undhiyu, chikki

Republic Day

Region: Pan-India

Republic Day commemorates the adoption of the Indian Constitution on 26 January 1950 and is celebrated with pride and parades.
