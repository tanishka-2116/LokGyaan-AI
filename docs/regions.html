Why Region Matters

In India, traditions are deeply influenced by geography, climate, language, and local history. The same festival or practice may have different meanings and customs across regions.

Examples of Regional Traditions

Maharashtra

Makar Sankranti is celebrated by exchanging Tilgul (sesame sweets), symbolizing harmony and warmth during winter.

Tamil Nadu

Pongal is a harvest festival thanking nature, farmers, and cattle, celebrated over four days with traditional cooking rituals.

Punjab

Lohri marks the winter harvest season and is celebrated with bonfires, folk songs, and community gatherings.
