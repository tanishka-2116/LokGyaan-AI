Who Can Contribute?

LokGyaan AI welcomes contributions from farmers, artisans, elders, cultural practitioners, and community members who wish to share traditional knowledge and lived experiences.

How Contributions Are Collected

Knowledge is shared voluntarily, primarily through spoken narratives in regional languages. These voices are then respectfully interpreted and structured using AI.

Ethical & Consent-Based Approach

  • Explicit consent is taken before recording or storing knowledge
  • Contributors and communities are respected and acknowledged
  • No commercialization without proper attribution
  • Knowledge is preserved for education, research, and social good

Our Commitment

LokGyaan AI is committed to preserving India’s traditional knowledge without distortion, exploitation, or loss of context. The platform exists to serve communities and future generations.
