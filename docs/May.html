May Festivals of India

May is the peak summer month in India. Festivals during this time focus on charity, spiritual balance, self-discipline, and cooling practices to protect the body and mind.

Akshaya Tritiya

Region

Pan-India

Historical Origin

Akshaya Tritiya is mentioned in ancient Hindu scriptures as a day when positive actions yield everlasting results (“Akshaya” means never diminishing). It is associated with Lord Vishnu and Goddess Lakshmi.

Why This Festival Exists

Summer was historically a difficult season. This festival encouraged hope, charity, and new beginnings even during physical hardship.

How It Is Celebrated

People perform charity, begin new ventures, conduct marriages, and worship Vishnu and Lakshmi.

Traditional Food Knowledge

Simple sattvic foods and hydration-focused meals help maintain digestive balance during extreme heat.

Traditional Clothing

Light cotton or silk attire in auspicious colors like yellow or gold.

Values Passed On

Generosity, optimism, faith in continuity of goodness.

Buddha Purnima

Region

Pan-India, especially Bihar and Uttar Pradesh

Historical Origin

Buddha Purnima commemorates the birth, enlightenment, and death (Mahaparinirvana) of Gautama Buddha.

Why This Festival Exists

It reinforces the principles of non-violence, compassion, and mindful living in society.

How It Is Celebrated

Buddhist monasteries conduct prayers, sermons, meditation sessions, and charity activities.

Traditional Food Knowledge

Vegetarian meals emphasize simplicity, moderation, and mental clarity.

Traditional Clothing

White or light-colored clothes symbolizing peace and purity.

Values Passed On

Ahimsa (non-violence), compassion, wisdom, mindfulness.

Narasimha Jayanti

Region

South India, Odisha, Maharashtra

Historical Origin

Narasimha Jayanti celebrates Lord Vishnu’s Narasimha avatar, who protected devotee Prahlad from injustice and tyranny.

Why This Festival Exists

It reinforces moral courage and faith during times of fear and social imbalance.

How It Is Celebrated

Devotees fast, recite Vishnu Sahasranama, and attend temple rituals.

Traditional Food Knowledge

Light fasting foods reduce bodily heat and promote self-discipline.

Traditional Clothing

Simple traditional attire in light colors.

Values Passed On

Faith, justice, protection of righteousness.
