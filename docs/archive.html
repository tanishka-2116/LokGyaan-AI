What is the Knowledge Archive?

The LokGyaan AI Knowledge Archive stores traditional and cultural knowledge in a structured digital format. Instead of plain text, information is organized by region, language, context, and practice.

What Kind of Knowledge is Stored?

  • Festival and cultural practices
  • Regional rituals and customs
  • Traditional food and seasonal relevance
  • Farming and harvest-related wisdom
  • Local terminology and language context

How Knowledge is Structured

Each knowledge entry includes details such as the region or state, local name, time period, cultural meaning, and associated practices. This structured format makes the information searchable, reusable, and suitable for education and research.

Why Structure Matters

Structured knowledge ensures that traditional wisdom is not lost in long narratives. It allows future systems, researchers, and communities to understand context, variation, and relevance clearly.
