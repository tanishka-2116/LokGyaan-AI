Makar Sankranti

Region: Maharashtra, Karnataka, UP, Bihar

Makar Sankranti marks the Sun’s northward movement and the end of winter. In Maharashtra, people exchange Tilgul and greet each other with “Tilgul ghya, god god bola”, symbolizing sweetness and harmony.

Food: Tilgul, puran poli

Clothing: Black sarees with colorful borders, dhoti-kurta

Traditional Knowledge: Seasonal nutrition and solar calendar awareness

Pongal

Region: Tamil Nadu

Pongal is a four-day harvest festival thanking the Sun God, farmers, cattle, and nature. Rice is cooked until it overflows, symbolizing abundance and prosperity.

Food: Sweet Pongal, Ven Pongal

Clothing: Veshti, silk and cotton sarees

Traditional Knowledge: Sustainable farming and gratitude to nature

Lohri

Region: Punjab, Haryana

Lohri is celebrated around community bonfires to mark the winter harvest. People sing folk songs, dance Bhangra and Gidda, and thank nature for agricultural prosperity.

Food: Revri, peanuts, popcorn

Clothing: Phulkari dupattas, turbans

Traditional Knowledge: Community bonding and seasonal awareness

Magh Bihu (Bhogali Bihu)

Region: Assam

Magh Bihu marks the end of the harvest season in Assam. Communities gather for feasts, traditional games, and celebrations focused on food and unity.

Food: Pitha, laru, rice-based dishes

Clothing: Mekhela chador, traditional attire

Traditional Knowledge: Food preservation and harvest cycles

Uttarayan

Region: Gujarat

Uttarayan celebrates the Sun’s northward journey and is famous for kite flying. Rooftops become social spaces filled with color, music, and joy.

Food: Undhiyu, chikki

Traditional Knowledge: Seasonal transition and solar movement

Paush Purnima

Region: North India

Paush Purnima is observed with holy river bathing, fasting, and charity. It marks spiritual purification and devotion.

Gangasagar Mela

Region: West Bengal

Gangasagar Mela is a major pilgrimage held at the confluence of the Ganga River and the Bay of Bengal during Makar Sankranti.

Vaikuntha Ekadashi

Region: Pan-India

Vaikuntha Ekadashi is dedicated to Lord Vishnu. Devotees fast, visit temples, and believe the gates of Vaikuntha open on this day.

Tusu Parab

Region: Jharkhand, West Bengal

Tusu Parab is a tribal harvest festival featuring folk songs, dances, and offerings to Goddess Tusu for prosperity.

Republic Day

Region: Pan-India

Republic Day commemorates the adoption of the Indian Constitution on 26 January 1950 and is celebrated with national pride and parades.
