January is one of the most culturally rich months in India. It marks harvest, the Sun’s transition, gratitude to nature, and strong community bonding across regions.

Major Harvest Festivals

Makar Sankranti

Celebrated across many states, Makar Sankranti marks the Sun’s northward journey and seasonal transition.

Regions: Maharashtra, Karnataka, MP, UP, Bihar

  • Vaikuntha Ekadashi – Vaishnav tradition
  • Tusu Parab – Tribal harvest festival (Jharkhand, Bengal)
  • Republic Day – National Festival of India (26 Jan)
