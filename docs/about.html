What is LokGyaan AI?

LokGyaan AI is a social-good platform designed to preserve India’s disappearing traditional and cultural knowledge. Much of this knowledge exists only in spoken form and is passed orally across generations.

The Problem We Are Solving

Rapid urbanization, modernization, and migration are causing the loss of valuable traditional wisdom related to culture, festivals, agriculture, seasonal practices, and community life. Existing digital platforms focus mostly on text and fail to capture regional context and oral knowledge.

How LokGyaan AI Works

  • Community members share traditional knowledge through voice
  • AI understands regional language and cultural context
  • Knowledge is converted into structured digital information
  • Users can explore traditions by region and category

Why Regional & Cultural Diversity Matters

The same tradition or festival can have different meanings, names, and practices across Indian states. LokGyaan AI preserves these regional variations instead of creating a single generalized version.

Ethical & Community-First Approach

LokGyaan AI follows an ethical, consent-based approach. Communities remain respected contributors, and knowledge is preserved for education, research, and social good — not exploitation.
