Festival Diversity in India

Indian festivals are deeply rooted in seasons, agriculture, climate, and regional culture. While the core idea may be shared, the rituals, food, and meanings vary across states.

Makar Sankranti and Its Regional Forms

Maharashtra

Known as Makar Sankranti, people exchange Tilgul sweets and wish each other harmony and sweetness.

Tamil Nadu

Celebrated as Pongal, it is a four-day harvest festival thanking the Sun God, farmers, and cattle.

Punjab

Celebrated as Lohri, it marks the winter harvest and is celebrated with bonfires, folk music, and dance.
