January Festivals Across India

January marks harvest, seasonal transition, and gratitude towards nature across different regions of India. Explore how the same season is celebrated differently across states.

Makar Sankranti (Maharashtra)

A winter harvest festival symbolizing harmony and warmth. Families exchange Tilgul and wear traditional black attire with colorful borders.

Food: Tilgul, puran poli

Pongal (Tamil Nadu)

A four-day harvest festival thanking the Sun, farmers, cattle, and nature. Rice overflowing in clay pots represents abundance.

Food: Sweet Pongal, Ven Pongal

Lohri (Punjab)

Celebrated around community bonfires marking the winter harvest. Music, dance, and collective joy define Lohri.

Food: Revri, peanuts, popcorn
