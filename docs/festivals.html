Makar Sankranti and Its Regional Forms

Maharashtra – Makar Sankranti

In Maharashtra, Makar Sankranti is celebrated as a festival of harmony and warmth during winter. Families exchange Tilgul made from sesame seeds and jaggery, symbolizing sweetness in relationships. Women often wear black sarees with colorful borders, traditionally believed to absorb winter warmth.

Food: Tilgul, puran poli
Theme: Harmony, seasonal transition

Tamil Nadu – Pongal

Pongal is a four-day harvest festival thanking the Sun God, farmers, cattle, and nature. The ritual of cooking rice until it overflows in clay pots represents abundance. Homes are decorated with kolam, and families wear traditional veshti and silk sarees.

Food: Sweet & Ven Pongal
Theme: Gratitude, harvest

Punjab – Lohri

Lohri marks the winter harvest and is celebrated around community bonfires. People sing folk songs, perform bhangra and gidda, and offer peanuts and revri to the fire. Traditional attire includes colorful phulkari shawls and turbans.

Food: Revri, peanuts, popcorn
Theme: Harvest, community bonding
