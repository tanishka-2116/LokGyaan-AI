India’s Living Knowledge

India’s traditions vary across regions, languages, seasons, and communities. LokGyaan AI preserves this cultural diversity using ethical and community-first AI.

Why LokGyaan AI?

Much of India’s traditional knowledge is passed orally and is rapidly disappearing. LokGyaan AI converts spoken wisdom into structured digital knowledge for future generations.

How It Works

  • Community members share knowledge through voice
  • AI understands language and regional context
  • Knowledge is structured and stored
  • Users explore traditions by region
