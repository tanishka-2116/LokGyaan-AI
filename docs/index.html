What is LokGyaan AI?

LokGyaan AI is a cultural knowledge platform designed to document and explain India’s living traditions in a structured, respectful, and region-aware manner. It focuses on preserving knowledge that has historically been passed orally across generations.

Why LokGyaan AI Matters

Preserving Oral Traditions

Many Indian traditions are not written down. LokGyaan AI helps document this knowledge before it disappears.

Respecting Regional Diversity

The same festival is celebrated differently across states. LokGyaan AI preserves these regional meanings and practices.

Education for Everyone

Designed for students, researchers, and the general public with simple language and clear explanations.
