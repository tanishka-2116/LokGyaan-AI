# LokGyaan AI

LokGyaan AI is an AI-powered initiative focused on preserving India’s oral traditional knowledge by converting spoken wisdom into structured and usable digital information for social good.

---

## 🌏 Problem Statement
A large part of India’s traditional and indigenous knowledge exists only in oral form and is passed across generations through experience and conversation. Due to urbanization and generational gaps, this valuable knowledge is rapidly disappearing and is not preserved in a structured or accessible way.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview
LokGyaan AI provides a platform where community members can share traditional knowledge through voice in regional Indian languages. Using AI techniques, the system understands the spoken input and organizes it into structured digital knowledge that can be preserved, searched, and reused responsibly.

The solution focuses on cultural preservation, inclusion, and ethical use of AI.

---

## ⚙️ Key Features
- Voice-based knowledge capture  
- Support for regional Indian languages  
- Extraction of steps, context, and conditions from speech  
- Structured storage of traditional knowledge  
- Ethical and consent-based data handling  

---

## 🧠 Technology Stack
- Python  
- FastAPI  
- Multilingual Speech-to-Text  
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)  
- Machine Learning  
- Knowledge Graphs  
- Cloud Infrastructure
